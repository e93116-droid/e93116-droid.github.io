Privacy Policy

How your information is handled

Effective: 17 October 2026 · Contact: e93116@gmail.com

Sean Personal Assistant is a privately operated, personal-use productivity tool. This policy explains how the assistant accesses, uses, stores and shares Google account information when the account owner chooses to connect Google services.

1. Information accessed

Depending on features explicitly authorised, the assistant may access Gmail message content, sender and recipient details, labels, attachments or drafts; Google Calendar events and availability; and contact information needed to address messages or arrange events. It may also process information the owner enters directly into the assistant.

2. Why it is used

Account data is used to respond to the owner's instructions: searching and organising mail, summarising messages or schedules, preparing or sending communications when separately authorised, finding contact details, and operating related personal workflows. It is not used to build an advertising profile or sold to advertisers.

3. Processing, storage and external services

Workflows may run on a computer controlled by the account owner, where necessary data, drafts, settings and operational logs may be stored. When an AI-assisted feature is used, relevant portions of authorised data may be transmitted securely to a configured AI service provider to perform the requested operation. Google APIs and other connected providers process data according to their own terms. This website itself is a static public information site and does not collect visitor account information or use analytics trackers.

4. Sharing and limits

Google user data is shared only as necessary to deliver the requested feature with the owner's selected service providers, or when required by law. It is not sold, shared with data brokers or used for personalised advertisements. Access by people outside the account owner is not provided as an ordinary feature. Any exceptional human access would require the owner's permission or a legal obligation.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs is intended to comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used to provide or improve user-facing features requested by the account owner, not for targeted advertising or unrelated model training.

6. Retention and deletion

Copies of account data, drafts, logs and generated summaries may be retained as required for an active workflow and its audit or troubleshooting needs. The owner can request deletion of locally retained copies, subject to necessary operational or legal retention. Revoking OAuth access stops future API access, but does not automatically delete existing local copies; those must be removed separately.

7. Your controls and security

The account owner controls what Google permissions are granted and can revoke access at any time in Google Account permissions. The assistant is not a public account-login service. Access to local computers and connected accounts should be protected by device security, separate credentials and least-privilege permissions. No internet service can promise absolute security.

8. Changes and contact

The policy may be updated if the assistant's features or data flows change; the effective date will be revised. Questions, privacy requests and concerns may be sent to e93116@gmail.com.
